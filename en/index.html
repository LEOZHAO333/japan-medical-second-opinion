From first conversation to a written opinion

  1. Online consultation

    We go over the diagnosis, treatment stage and what matters most to you, and check whether a Japanese specialist opinion fits.

  2. Written specialist opinion

    We organize pathology, imaging and treatment records and translate them into Japanese. A specialist at a relevant Japanese hospital writes the opinion, delivered in Japanese with a Chinese translation about 10–15 working days after the records are complete.

    Fees, timing and refunds (Chinese)

  3. Care coordination in Japan

    If you do need treatment in Japan and a hospital accepts you, we help with appointments, interpreting and travel. A second opinion is not a transfer of care or a promise of treatment.

What we don't do

Is JapanMedAI a hospital?

No. We organize Japanese medical information and cross-border care steps. We don't diagnose, prescribe or decide treatment.

Will a second opinion give me a new treatment plan?

Not necessarily. The specialist may confirm the current plan, point out missing information or discuss other options. The opinion is theirs and may match your current plan.

Is BNCT or iPS right for everyone?

No. Approved treatment, trials, research and individual cases differ, and the receiving institution checks eligibility.

Does the weight quiz recommend medication?

No. It only helps you look at habits. It doesn't diagnose, assess medication or sell prescriptions.
