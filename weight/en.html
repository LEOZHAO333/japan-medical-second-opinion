WEIGHT MANAGEMENT GUIDE · REVIEWED 2026-09-29

What can this 60-second check-in do?

It organizes self-reported habit cues—such as appetite, sleep, sedentary time, and weight regain—into a simple reflection prompt and one small action to consider. It is not a medical assessment, does not diagnose an eating or weight condition, predict weight change, or determine whether a medicine is appropriate.

Scope: This content is for adults aged 18 and older. People under 18 should not use adult BMI or weight-loss targets; discuss concerns with a guardian and a qualified clinician. Do not post weight, records, or identifying health information publicly. This tool does not prescribe, sell, or supply medicines.

Communication reference: WHO World Mental Health Day 2026 (checked 2026-09-29; campaign date: 2026-10-10). It is not a weight-management guideline. We borrow only its emphasis on listening to lived experience, without equating body weight with a mental-health diagnosis.

FAQ

Weight-management check-in: FAQs

What does a result mean?A prompt based on selected habits—not a diagnosis, personality diagnosis, or forecast.
Can it tell me whether a GLP-1 or other prescription medicine is right for me?No. A licensed clinician must assess indications, medical history, contraindications, and monitoring. This tool does not recommend or provide prescription medicines.
Why start with one small habit?That is a self-reflection design choice, not a clinical efficacy claim. Ask a health professional if an activity or goal may not fit your health situation.
Are results used for medical decisions?No. The tool is for personal reflection and has no medical-record upload field. Avoid entering or publicly sharing identifiable health information.
